1 What we did

The pilot tested, on a small scale, whether data about palm oil growers can be collected from public sources, built into tables, and traced back to where every value came from.

The question

Can the whole chain be built once, from raw public sources to a small table, with a record of where every value came from? The pilot counts as a success if every step from download to table can be followed and repeated, even where a measure could not be built, as long as the missing piece is written down.

The scope

  • 3 palm oil growers listed on the stock exchange: Sarawak Oil Palms Berhad (SOP), Ta Ann Holdings Berhad (Ta Ann), Sarawak Plantation Berhad (SPB).
  • Their 12 palm oil mills: SOP 7, Ta Ann 3, SPB 2.
  • A single region: the state of Sarawak, in Malaysia.
  • The years 2021–2023.

How the firms were chosen

Sarawak has 5 growers listed on the stock exchange whose mills are all in the state. They were compared on how much of their data was public and usable, and 3 were chosen.

What was measured and how

8 measures (the pilot calls them features) in 6 groups, which the pilot calls families: land and maps, climate, dependence on nature, operations, rules and bans, and finance (section 4). The work ran in 10 steps, and each step was checked and approved before the next step started.

What came out

  • A firm-year table with 9 rows: each firm in each year.
  • A table of values that do not change by year, with 3 rows.
  • A grid of 1 km squares covering Sarawak.
  • An evidence record for each of the 207 values in both tables.

The short answer

6 of the 8 features were built: 1 without a warning flag and 5 with flags. 2 are gaps. In the pilot’s words, the data turned out to be “less usable than it is collectable” (section 8).

Keep in mind. This is a test of the method, not a dataset for use. The firms were chosen because their data was available; they do not stand for the industry. The findings are waiting for a decision (section 9).

3 From raw data to the final tables, step by step

The data moved from raw downloads, through cut-down copies and results for each firm, to the final tables. Each time the scripts were run again, they produced exactly the same files.

The diagram in words
  1. Raw sources (data/raw/, saved in steps 2 and 5): Hansen forest change, CHIRPS rainfall, the Sarawak boundary, the firms’ mill lists, the Universal Mill List, eMSPO and RSPO records, 9 annual reports, the US and EU sanctions lists, ENCORE, OpenStreetMap roads. The CIFOR peat map could not be obtained. Sentinel-2 images and the Global Forest Watch map were used only for checks; the palm map only for pictures.
  2. Clips and extracts (data/interim/, step 5): the forest and rainfall layers cut to Sarawak, the 12 mills, the figures typed from the reports with their pages, certificate records, sanctions searches, the ENCORE extract, the roads. No peat clip.
  3. Results per firm (data/processed/, step 6): the catchments drawn from 90 mill points, then forest loss and rainfall by mill, certification by mill and year, yield, debt-to-equity, the sanctions search.
  4. Final files (steps 7–9): pilot_firm_year.csv (9 rows), pilot_static.csv (3 rows), the grid of Sarawak (125,982 squares), and evidence.csv (207 rows, each pointing to the source register, a method card, a script and a check result).
  1. Raw downloads (data/raw/, logged in data/raw_files.csv): saved once and never edited.
  2. Clips and extracts (data/interim/): the satellite and rainfall layers cut to Sarawak (tree cover, year of loss, palm extent from 22 map tiles, and monthly rainfall for 1991–2023: 396 monthly layers, no missing months); the mill tables; the figures typed from the annual reports, with their page numbers; certificate records; sanctions searches; the ENCORE extract; the roads.
  3. Results per firm (data/processed/, a set of files for each firm): forest loss by mill, rainfall by mill, certification by mill and year, yield, debt-to-equity and the sanctions search; then a firm-year table and a static table for each firm.
  4. Final tables:
    • pilot_firm_year.csv: 9 rows × 9 columns. 54 values: 45 filled, 9 missing.
    • pilot_static.csv: 3 rows × 53 columns. 153 values, of which 3 are missing.
    • evidence.csv: 207 rows × 19 columns.
    • The grid (section 5).

How missing values are written

As an empty cell, never as zero, and each has a recorded reason. “NO_DEPENDENCY” is an ENCORE rating (“no dependency”), not a missing value.

Why a separate static table

Values that do not change by year (peat, ENCORE) are kept apart, so that such a value is not repeated as if it were measured every year.

Tools and records

  • 33a Python programs, 4 scripts that run them in order, and 2 lists of the software they need.
  • Rebuilding the firm tables gave 104 identical outputs in 3 builds; rebuilding the grid gave 10 identical outputs in 3 builds.
  • 9 method cards (a short note per measure on how it was made); a list that describes each measure and each grid column (23 rows, 15 of them for the grid); a list of checks with 20 rows per firm.
  • A log of 58 questions that needed a decision: 56 decided.

Keep in mind. The raw files are not published. The grid has no evidence record for each value; it is described by its column list and its method card instead R053.

  • a Count at the time of the findings report.

4 The measures (features)

Each firm is described by a set of measures. Most could be built, and each comes with the pilot’s own warning about what it can and cannot show.

First, mill catchments

Forest loss and rainfall are measured around each firm’s mills. Each mill gets the land that is closer to it than to any other mill point (a “tessellation”), and the map is cut at the Sarawak border. The points are 90 mill points: the firms’ 12 mills and 78a points from the Universal Mill List (list points within 2 kma of a firm mill were dropped). A firm’s catchments are merged into a single area: SOP 26,529.84 km², Ta Ann 4,782.52 km², SPB 543.83 km².

  • a Universal Mill List points are unverified, and no licence is stated for the list (R023, R021).

Forest loss

Verdict: Built with flags

What it is. Hectares of tree-cover loss each year inside the firm’s merged catchment, where “forest” means more than 30% tree cover in 2000.

How it was checked. 18 of 18 yearly values matched the Global Forest Watch website, which shows only the first 2 digits of each number (differences −1.5% to +1.8%), for 2 catchments per firm. Satellite close-ups of the loss areas, in the pilot’s words: 6 pass, 2 partly plausible.

The pilot’s main reason: Hansen also counts replanting and logging. Metanik's catchment dominates SOP's value (R040). The GFW check covers 2 cells per firm at 2 significant figures

  • – Loss from any cause inside the catchment area, including replanting and logging; not necessarily land cleared by the firm.

Peat overlap

Verdict: Gap

What it is. The share of the catchment that lies on peat.

How it was checked. Not checked: the peat map could not be downloaded, and no substitute was used.

The pilot’s main reason: Peat layer could not be obtained (R034). No substitute

  • – Missing: the peat map could not be obtained, and no substitute was used (R034).

Rainfall anomaly

Verdict: Built

What it is. Each year’s rainfall minus the 1991–2020 average for the same place, in mm, averaged over the firm’s mills.

How it was checked. A second, separate calculation found 0 of 396 mill-years that differed by more than 2 mm.

The pilot’s main reason: All checks pass. Every mill has data in every year

ENCORE

Verdict: Built with flags

What it is. 50ab ratings of how much an activity depends on nature: 25a kinds of benefit from nature (ENCORE calls them “ecosystem services”) × 2a activities (growing perennial crops; making vegetable and animal oils and fats), from very high to very low, or no dependency. ENCORE content, CC BY-SA 4.0

How it was checked. ENCORE’s ratings file and its detail file agree for all 50/50 ratings, and all 150 values in the tables passed their check.

The pilot’s main reason: Activity-level proxy, identical for all 3 firms (R036)

  • a ENCORE content, CC BY-SA 4.0, shown with the ENCORE citation.
  • b An activity-level proxy: every firm gets the same ratings, from the May 2026 version of ENCORE, not 2021–2023 (R036).

Certification

Verdict: Built with flags

What it is. The share of the firm’s mills that were certified (MSPO or RSPO) on 31 December of each year.

How it was checked. eMSPO records equal the live website (63/63 and 45/45 fields); 9/9 pass. These checks test the method; the evidence itself is weak (section 8).

The pilot’s main reason: 100% in all 9 firm-years. 20 of 36 mill-years rest on the firms' own statements (R038)

  • – Includes mill-years that rest on the firms' own group-level statements in their annual reports, not on certificate records (R038).

FFB yield

Verdict: Built with flags

What it is. Tonnes of fresh fruit bunches (FFB) harvested per hectare, exactly as each annual report prints it.

How it was checked. 9/9 pass; 1 restatement found.

The pilot’s main reason: Units and denominators differ between firms (R035, R048). Ta Ann restated its 2022 yield (R046)

  • – Units and hectare bases differ between firms, so yields cannot be compared between firms (R048).

WRO/sanctions

Verdict: Gap

What it is. The number of import bans (US Withhold Release Orders) and EU financial sanctions in force against the firm.

How it was checked. Searches of the current US and EU lists found no match, but the lists do not show 2021–2023, so the count is missing.

The pilot’s main reason: No 2021–2023 history, so the count is missing in all 9 firm-years (R020). Current lists checked: no match

  • – Missing, not zero: the official lists hold only current entries, so 2021–2023 could not be checked. The current lists show no match (R020).

Debt-to-equity

Verdict: Built with flags

What it is. Borrowings divided by total equity, for the whole group, from each year’s annual report.

How it was checked. Every figure that is not zero was found again on its report page (63/63 (SOP), 60/60 (Ta Ann), 46/46 (SPB)). All 9/9 values pass.

The pilot’s main reason: Group figures include non-palm business (R007). Borrowing line items are named differently across firms (R047)

  • – Whole-group figure, including business outside palm oil (R007).

5 The grid of Sarawak

A grid of square cells covers all of Sarawak, with yearly forest loss and the distances to the nearest mill and road in each square, and its total matches the state-wide total.

The grid has 125,982 squares of 1 km. 4,111 of them are edge squares, cut by the border. Together they cover 123,769.32 km². Each square has 15 columns, including forest loss in 2021, 2022 and 2023 (ha), the distance to the nearest mill point and to the nearest road (km), and peat, which is empty in all 125,982a squares.

6 The evidence trail, with a worked example

Every value in both tables has an evidence row that leads to its source page, method, script and check. Here a single value is followed link by link.

An evidence row has 19 columns. In plain words: the value; the source and the exact place in it (page, table or map layer); the unit; the date it refers to; the area it covers; the method card; the script; how it was checked, the result, who checked it and when; and a note.

  • 195a values pass their check and 0 fail.
  • 12 are “not checked”: these are the missing values (9b import-ban and sanctions values, 3c peat values).
  • All 195a checks were automated self-checks. The planned manual reviews were waived, so no value was checked independently (section 8).
  • a All checks were self-checks, done by the same party that built the values; no value was checked independently (R043).
  • b Missing, not zero: the official lists hold only current entries, so 2021–2023 could not be checked. The current lists show no match (R020).
  • c Missing: the peat map could not be obtained, and no substitute was used (R034).

A worked example: SOP, debt-to-equity, 2022 = 0.2207a

The value is followed through 7 links. Amounts are in thousands of Malaysian ringgit, as the report prints them.

  1. The value in the table: 0.2207a, in data/processed/firm1_firm_year.csv (copied unchanged into the combined table pilot_firm_year.csv), row SOP 2022, column “debt_to_equity”.
  2. Its evidence row: value 0.2207a; source: the SOP Annual Report 2022; reference date 31 December 2022; area: the whole group.
  3. The source: registered in the source register and downloaded on 23 October 2026, 07:01:46 UTC. A digital fingerprint of the file (SHA-256) was calculated again and still matches, so the file has not changed.
  4. The pages: the balance sheet (page 151 of the file; printed page 149) shows loans and borrowings of 446,125 RM'000 (non-current) and 314,829 RM'000 (current), and total equity of 3,447,314 RM'000. The borrowings note (page 215 of the file; printed page 213) gives the line items.
  5. The method card: hire purchase (1,875 RM'000) counts as debt; lease liabilities (1,550 RM'000) are left out. 760,954 RM'000 ÷ 3,447,314 RM'000 = 0.220738, shown as 0.2207a.
  6. The scripts that compute and record it: scripts/step6_financials.py and scripts/step7_firm_year_table.py.
  7. The check: pass (an automated check), on 24 October 2026. The trace was re-run on 24 October 2026 and passed again.

The chain is the same for other kinds of value. Map and rainfall values lead to a map layer and a result row; certification leads to the eMSPO record or to the annual-report statement; a missing value leads to a row that gives the reason.

Keep in mind. The annual report itself is not published here; it is cited by title, year and page.

Catchment
The land that belongs to a mill on the map: every place closer to that mill than to any other mill point. A firm’s catchment is all its mills’ catchments merged.
Tessellation
The way the catchments are drawn: the map is split into areas, an area for each mill point, holding the places nearest to that point.
Mill point
The map position of a palm oil mill, from the firms and eMSPO, or from the Universal Mill List.
Universal Mill List (UML)
A public list of palm oil mill positions (Rainforest Alliance; WRI/GFW). Unverified; no licence stated.
Feature
A single measure in the tables, for example forest loss or debt-to-equity.
Family
A group of features: geospatial, climate, dependency, operational, regulatory, financial.
Firm-year
A firm in a given year: a row of the main table.
Static value
A value that does not change by year (peat overlap, ENCORE), kept in its own table.
Evidence row
A line in the evidence file for a single value: where it came from, how it was made, how it was checked.
Source tag
On this page: the file, row and column in the pilot where a number comes from.

A feature that could not be built. Recorded, not filled.
Flag
A warning the pilot attached to a value.
Missing
An empty value with a recorded reason. Not zero.
Not checked
The check status of a missing value.
Self-check
A check done by the same party that built the value, not by an independent reviewer.
Tree-cover loss (Hansen)
Satellite-detected loss of tree cover from any cause, including clearing, replanting and logging.
Rainfall anomaly
A year’s rainfall minus the 1991–2020 average for the same place, in mm.
ENCORE rating
How much an activity depends on a part of nature: very high to very low, or no dependency.
NO_DEPENDENCY
ENCORE’s “N/A”: the activity does not depend on that service. A rating, not a missing value.
MSPO, RSPO, eMSPO
Palm oil certification schemes (Malaysian Sustainable Palm Oil; Roundtable on Sustainable Palm Oil), and MSPO’s online list of certified sites.
Mill-year
A mill in a given year.
Firm-reported
Based on the firm’s own statement in its annual report, not on a certificate record.

Fresh fruit bunches harvested per hectare, as the firm reports it.
mt, mt/ha
Tonnes; tonnes per hectare, as printed in the annual report.

How SPB prints its yield: tonnes per “weighted average hectarage”, its harvestable area (the pilot’s method card for FFB yield).
AR
Annual report, as in “AR” followed by the year of the report.
Debt-to-equity
Borrowings divided by total equity, for the whole group.
Restatement
A figure that a later annual report prints differently for the same year.
WRO
Withhold Release Order: a US order that stops certain imports.
Grid square, edge square
A square of the grid; an edge square is cut by the Sarawak border.
